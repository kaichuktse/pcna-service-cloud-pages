Overview

Vonage replaces Five9, and Casey replaces Clara.

PCNA is replacing Five9 with Vonage as its contact-center platform, and its current AI agent, Clara, is being retired. This program uses that switch to put Casey, a Salesforce AI agent, on phone and web chat. Casey answers from a new Knowledge base, tells customers where their order is, and hands off to a CSR with the full context. The 100 CSRs work from one Service Cloud console with AI assistance, and case teams are organized by territory pod. Everything here is a working assumption from PCNA's reverse demo until PCNA answers the shared scoping questions.

P0 · Mobilization and Integration Design

Name the order systems and confirm API access. Complete the joint design with Vonage and set the cutover date.

P1 · Foundations: Telephony, Orders and Knowledge

Connect Vonage to Salesforce. Build order lookup across all three systems. Set up the Knowledge base and case teams.

P2 · Casey Agent and AI Grounding

Build Casey: case handling, Knowledge answers, identity checks, order status and handoff to a CSR. Ground the AI on Knowledge and connect Outlook.

P3 · Channels and CSR Productivity AI

Put Casey on phone and web chat. Launch web-to-case. Pilot the CSR AI features with CSRs.

P4 · Stabilization and Cutover

End-to-end testing with PCNA users and Vonage. Go live on the Vonage cutover date and hand over to PCNA admins.

P5 · Hypercare

Two weeks of production support, fixing issues and tuning Casey on real conversations.

Phase 0, cutover and hypercare stay the same length in every delivery model: PCNA and Vonage set their pace, and go-live aligns to the Vonage cutover date. The faster models reach go-live sooner with a team of similar size.

AI-native is conditional. It holds only if PCNA commits to the way of working: an empowered product owner who decides quickly, daily build-and-review cycles, an executive mandate for the approach, and approval of AI tools on PCNA data and environments. None is confirmed today.

Next steps

Four actions start Phase 0.

  1. Answer the priority questions: the two further order systems and their APIs, the Vonage cutover date and SIP-partner confirmation, the Knowledge inventory and authors.
  2. Name PCNA's product owner, sponsor, and change and training owner.
  3. Choose a delivery model.
  4. Confirm the Phase 0 start; pricing follows.
