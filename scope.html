Phase 0, cutover and hypercare stay the same length in every delivery model: PCNA and Vonage set their pace, and go-live aligns to the Vonage cutover date. The faster models reach go-live sooner with a team of similar size.

AI-native is conditional. It holds only if PCNA commits to the way of working: an empowered product owner who decides quickly, daily build-and-review cycles, an executive mandate for the approach, and approval of AI tools on PCNA data and environments. None is confirmed today.

G0005Missing RequirementOrder systems: besides FDM4, which two other order management systems does Casey need to read from, and what API access exists for each?
G0006Missing RequirementKnowledge: where do current articles, FAQs and policy documents live, how many and what types, are they current, and who owns authoring?
G0007Missing RequirementScale: number of CSRs, supervisors, brands/sites and languages for web-to-case, webchat and Casey.
G0008Missing RequirementTarget go-live date, and does it depend on the Vonage cutover date?
G0009Potential RiskOutlook: should escalation emails be stored on the case in Salesforce? Einstein Activity Capture stores captured email off-core unless 'Sync Email as Salesforce Activity' is enabled.
G0010AmbiguityClara (current customer-facing AI agent) is being retired. What platform does Clara run on today, what does it handle, and does any of its content or intent design carry into Casey?

G0014Missing RequirementWhat data will the Vonage IVR pass to Salesforce besides the caller's number (ANI), and does the console need screen pops beyond the Contact/Account match?
G0701Logical GapCase teams don't stop cherry-picking on their own. Cherry-picking happens when reps can choose cases: any queue member can take ownership of cases from a queue list view. If the goal is to stop cherry-picking, cases have to be assigned to reps (pushed), not left in queues for reps to pull. Who assigns PCNA's non-voice cases (email, web-to-case, escalated child cases) to a named rep: Vonage external routing, Salesforce Omni-Channel, or assignment rules? And should reps lose the ability to pick cases from queues?
G0702Missing RequirementParent/child behavior: when a CSR escalates today by cloning a case, which fields does the clone carry over, who owns the child case (another case type's queue, such as Credit Team or Post-Prod Order Mgmt?), and what rules link the two? For example: can the parent close while children are open, does a child's closure update the parent, and is customer communication sent from the parent only?
G0703Potential RiskSLAs are 'unchanged', but child cases are new records. Will the existing entitlement processes and milestones fire on child cases, measure the parent only, or both? Will today's SLA and case-volume reports double-count once escalations become child cases instead of clones?
G0704Missing RequirementCase team roles and access: which roles does a territory pod need (for example pod lead, CSR, Credit or Inside Sales collaborator), and does each get read-only or read/write access? Should the pod team be added to cases automatically when the case matches the pod's territory, or added manually by reps?
G0705AmbiguityExisting cloned cases: at go-live, should the open cloned escalation cases be linked into parent/child relationships, or does the new model apply only to escalations created after go-live?
G0706Missing RequirementCase sharing model: what is the Case org-wide default today (Private, Public Read Only, or Public Read/Write/Transfer), and are there role or territory hierarchies in use? Pod-based case teams only restrict or extend access in a meaningful way if the baseline sharing is more restrictive than the team's access.
G0801Potential RiskIs Classic Knowledge (or any Knowledge setting) already enabled in PCNA's existing org, even if unused? Turning on Lightning Knowledge changes the data model to record types and can't be undone.
G0802Missing RequirementArticle audiences and visibility: which articles are customer-safe (Casey can quote them to callers and chat visitors) and which are internal to CSRs (pricing exceptions, credit policy, vendor terms)? How should data categories be structured: by case type, product line, brand, or audience?
G0803Logical GapArticle field design has to follow the grounding rules. Will PCNA's article template keep the AI-indexed fields in plain text or long text area? Rich-text bodies with formatting, tables or embedded images may not be indexed as grounding content.
G0804AmbiguityApproval process: is a single-stage approval (author, then one Knowledge approver, then publish) enough, or does PCNA need multiple review stages (for example a legal or credit-policy reviewer for policy articles, and an extra review for AI-drafted articles from E10)?
G0805Missing RequirementInitial content load: who turns PCNA's existing FAQs and policy documents into structured Knowledge articles (PCNA authors, Salesforce PS, or a bulk import from a source system), what format is the source content in, and how many articles must be published before Casey and Service Assistant can go live?
G0806Missing RequirementContent design: does PCNA have anyone who writes for a knowledge base (a content designer or knowledge manager), or will articles be written by CSRs? Does PCNA want help defining article templates, a style guide (question-led titles, one answer per article), and a knowledge-centered service (KCS) approach so CSRs capture knowledge from cases?
G0807Missing RequirementKnowledge governance after go-live: who owns the knowledge base (a named knowledge manager or Center of Excellence), how often are articles reviewed for accuracy, who archives outdated policy articles, and how are the Knowledge gaps that Casey and Service Assistant reveal fed back to authors?
