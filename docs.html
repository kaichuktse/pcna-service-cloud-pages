Executive summary

PCNA Service Cloud 2026: Executive Summary

Prepared for: PCNA executive sponsors and steering committee · Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: Draft for discussion. The scope rests on working assumptions from PCNA's reverse demo; the shared scoping questions are open.

At a Glance

  • Current state: PCNA is replacing Five9 with Vonage. The current AI agent, Clara, is being retired, and Salesforce has no knowledge base to answer customers or support CSRs.
  • Vision: Casey, a Salesforce AI agent, answers customers on phone and web chat, tells them where their order is, and hands off to a CSR with the full context. 100 CSRs work from one Service Cloud console with AI assistance.
  • What this delivers:
  • customer self-service on voice and chat
  • faster CSR resolution with AI-drafted replies and summaries
  • case teams organized by territory pod
  • Biggest unknown: the three order systems Casey reads from. Only FDM4 is named, and API access to the others is unconfirmed.
  • Recommended first step: a 3-week Phase 0 that names the order systems, completes the joint design with Vonage, and confirms the Vonage cutover date.

Overview

PCNA's move from Five9 to Vonage sets the timing for its service operation. Open CTI, the older Salesforce telephony integration, is being retired, so the Vonage connection goes through Salesforce Voice partner telephony. The same switch is the moment to replace Clara with Casey. Casey runs on Salesforce, uses PCNA's own knowledge articles to answer customers, and reads live order status.

Scope

All work happens in PCNA's existing Service Cloud org. No data migration is needed, and case types and service-level agreements stay as they are.

Area What PCNA gets
Casey An AI agent on phone (calls arrive from Vonage) and live web chat. It creates and updates cases, answers from Knowledge, verifies identity, reports where an order is, and transfers to a CSR.
Order lookup Read-only order status from FDM4 and two further order systems.
Web channels Live chat fronted by Casey, and a web-to-case form.
Case management Parent/child cases in place of cloning, and case teams by territory pod.
Knowledge A new Salesforce Knowledge base with an approval process. CSR policy files become internal articles.
CSR productivity AI Service Assistant, Service Replies, Work Summary, Conversation Catch-up, AI-drafted articles and recommendations, and Next Best Action.
Vonage, Salesforce side Partner telephony, mirrored queues and users, updated CSR and supervisor consoles.
Outlook Escalation emails attached to the case.

Vonage owns: IVR, call and email routing, recording, transcription, the dialer, workforce management and the telephony build.

PCNA owns: - writing the Knowledge content - exposing the order-system APIs - UAT testers - change management and CSR classroom training

Solution Highlights

  • Partner telephony with Vonage over SIP, the internet calling standard. Calls reach Casey with full call context, and Vonage remains the single router for voice and email. This depends on Vonage confirming in writing that it is a supported SIP partner.
  • One order lookup across all three systems. Casey answers "where is my order?" without needing to know which system holds the order.
  • Knowledge grounds the AI. Casey and the CSR assistive features answer from PCNA-approved articles, so the quality and timing of PCNA's content directly shape AI quality at go-live.

Implementation Approach

34 weeks: 32 weeks to a single go-live aligned to the Vonage cutover, then 2 weeks of hypercare.

Effort Summary

  • Complexity: of the twelve work areas, two are large (order lookup and CSR productivity AI), seven medium and three small. Casey's standard agent is being built and tested by a Salesforce team as a proof of concept before this project starts. This project takes it over, adds the order subtopic and runs regression testing.
  • Salesforce team: a senior Solution Architect and a dedicated Agentforce Technical Architect lead, with a project manager, an onshore integration developer for the order systems, and offshore configuration, development and testing.
  • PCNA team: a product owner, a sponsor, CSR and supervisor experts, IT support for the order-system APIs, Knowledge authors, UAT testers, and a change and training owner. None of these roles is named yet.

Investment is to be confirmed once commercial terms are set.

Solution

PCNA Service Cloud 2026 — Solution

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: Design. Every decision rests on Salesforce team assumptions made from PCNA's reverse demo. PCNA's answers to the scoping questions are pending.

Summary

PCNA is replacing Five9 with Vonage Contact Center (CC360). Salesforce extends PCNA's existing Service Cloud org around that change:

  • Casey. An Agentforce Service Agent that answers customers on voice and web chat, looks up orders, and hands off to a CSR.
  • Knowledge. A new Salesforce Knowledge base that grounds Casey and the CSR AI features.
  • CSR AI. Agentforce assistive features for 100 CSRs.
  • Case management. Parent/child cases and pod-based case teams.
  • Vonage on the Salesforce side. The CSR and supervisor consoles move to Vonage.

Vonage owns IVR, call and email routing, recording, transcription, the dialer and workforce management.

Three dependencies shape the whole design: - Vonage is assumed to be a supported Agentforce Voice SIP partner (G0002). Vonage must confirm it in Phase 0. - Vonage and Salesforce must agree on routing and CSR availability (G0003, G0202, G0503). - PCNA must supply the Knowledge content (G0006).

Solution by Business Process

Epics are ordered by the customer's journey: contact, then self-service, then a CSR, then resolution. Mobilization, the Vonage platform work and the program epics come last.

E05 — Web Channels: Live Chat and Web-to-Case

Business context: PCNA has no web chat or web form today; customers use email or phone. PCNA wants digital self-service that Casey answers first.

Solution approach: - Live chat: Messaging for In-App and Web (Enhanced Chat) on PCNA's brand sites, fronted by Casey, with escalation to CSRs. - Web-to-case: a standard web-to-case form for customers who want to log an issue without chatting.

Supporting architecture: - Chat build. Enhanced Chat v2: a messaging channel connected to Casey's inbound Omni-Channel flow, with a required fallback queue for escalation. Choose v1 instead only if PCNA needs a pre-chat form or a post-chat survey (G0502). - Identity on chat. Chat starts anonymous. Casey answers Knowledge questions without verification and asks the customer to verify before it shows order or case data. The method is decided with E03 (G0301, G0504). - Web form. The standard Web-to-Case form, plus reCAPTCHA. An Experience Cloud Create Case Form only if PCNA wants a logged-in portal (G0505). - Routing. Omni-Channel routes chat escalations and web-to-case cases to CSR queues. How that coexists with Vonage is the open availability question in Foundations.

E04 — Casey Voice Channel

Business context: PCNA wants Casey as the voice front door in place of Clara. The touch-tone IVR stays in Vonage.

Solution approach: Vonage sends calls to Casey over SIP through Agentforce Voice. Casey either resolves the call or hands it back to Vonage, which routes it to a CSR with the call context.

Supporting architecture: - Inbound. A SIP-over-TLS trunk runs between Vonage's session border controller and Salesforce's, and passes full call context. Vonage builds and owns the trunk. Salesforce PS builds the Salesforce channel, the inbound and outbound Omni-Channel flows and the escalation step. - Escalation. The call returns to Vonage by SIP transfer, with custom SIP headers carrying the reason and summary. Vonage creates the first call record and passes its ID, so Casey's and the CSR's call records stay linked. How Vonage handles its side of this is (G0401, G0403). - SIP partner status is assumed (G0002). If Vonage confirms otherwise, the only option is Dynamic Routing, which can't record voice calls. That would reopen ADR 0001 and make E04 an L. - Voice design. Voice turns are short and tolerate interruption. Order-system calls must answer within the voice response budget (G0605).

E03 — Casey: Handover, Order Subtopic and Production Readiness

Business context: Clara, PCNA's current AI agent, is retiring. The Salesforce team is building and testing Casey, the out-of-the-box Agentforce Service Agent, as a proof of concept in a sandbox. We assume it is complete and tested before PS starts (G0021).

Solution approach: - Built by a Salesforce team, not PS: the standard actions. These are create a case, update a case, check case status, answer from Knowledge, verify identity and transfer to a CSR. - Handover and promotion (PS). Accept the handover, put Casey's metadata under source control and promote it to production through PCNA's pipeline. - "Where is my order?" subtopic (PS). Added after the handover, on the E06 order action. - Regression testing (PS). Covers the handed-over actions and the new subtopic, and is repeated once Casey is on voice and chat.

Supporting architecture: - One agent, two channels. Voice and web chat are separate connections on the same agent, each with its own inbound and outbound Omni-Channel flows and a shared escalation subagent. - Actions. Deterministic Flow or Apex actions for case work and order lookup; prompt-based answers only for Knowledge. Each action is small and has defined inputs and outputs. - Identity verification. A verified tier controls access to orders and cases (for example email one-time code, or account number plus a second factor). The method is PCNA's call (G0301). - Guardrails. Casey hands off on pricing, credit, pre-sales, inside sales and anything it can't commit to (G0305). A test set and pass criteria are agreed before testing starts (G0307). - POC promotion. We assume PS promotes the POC as built, without rebuilding it. Phase 0 confirms its metadata and source-control state. Grounding libraries from the sandbox must be rebuilt in each environment (G0011, G0308, G0021).

E06 — Order Lookup Integration

Business context: Most service contacts are order questions. Order data lives in FDM4 and two other, unnamed order systems.

Solution approach: Casey answers "Where is my order?" through live, read-only lookups: it finds the customer's order and reports its status. Casey does not create orders (working assumption, 2026-10-13; G0102 resolved).

Supporting architecture: - Live calls, no copy. Casey calls each order system in real time. No order data is copied into Salesforce or Data Cloud. Keeping a copy is the alternative if an order system can't answer fast enough. , no source covers it (G0103). - Pattern. One standard order-status action for Casey, with a small adapter per order system. - How each adapter is built: Apex or External Services, using secure stored credentials (Named Credentials). - MuleSoft only if PCNA already runs it. Three order systems is the usual point where middleware pays off, so this is worth confirming in Phase 0 (G0602). - Finding the order. Casey has to work out which of the three systems holds the order, whether by order-number format, brand or order type, or by querying all three (G0603). What it returns is still open: status, ship date, carrier and tracking, production milestones (G0606). - Customer matching. Each order system needs an ID that links a verified Salesforce Contact or Account to its orders (G0604). - Reuse.** Service Assistant (E10) reuses these same actions. No separate CSR integration is built (G1005).

E07 — Case Management Enhancements

Business context: CSRs clone a case to escalate it, which breaks the history. Cases sit open in queues, so reps cherry-pick the easy ones.

Solution approach: - Parent/child cases. An escalation creates a child case linked to the parent. - Pod case teams. Territory-based case teams are added to cases automatically.

Supporting architecture: - Parent/child. The standard Parent Case lookup, with a quick action or Flow to create the child and carry fields across. Child cases are checked against entitlement processes so SLAs aren't counted twice (G0703). - Pods. Predefined case teams with roles and access levels, added by assignment Flow. Pod design depends on how PCNA defines territories (G0012). - Cherry-picking. Case teams grant access but don't assign work. Stopping cherry-picking needs push routing (Omni-Channel or Vonage) instead of reps pulling from list views. This is decided together with the routing split (G0701, G0003).

E08 — Knowledge Management

Business context: PCNA has no Knowledge in Salesforce. CSR FAQs and policies sit in documents.

Solution approach: - Setup. Lightning Knowledge with two article types, customer FAQ and internal policy and procedure. - Organization. Data categories, a creation and approval process, and support for loading PCNA's initial content. - Policy files. CSR policy and FAQ files are converted into internal articles.

Supporting architecture: - Why convert the files. Service Assistant grounds only on Knowledge and can use only one data library. A separate file library could serve Casey but not CSRs (G0901). - Field design. Fields used for AI are text or text area (Title, a short Summary, plus content fields). Indexing rich-text fields is unconfirmed and gets a sandbox check (G0803). - Visibility. A public/internal split by data category and visibility, so Casey's library can be limited to customer-safe content (G0802). - Approval. A standard approval process. Flow Orchestration only if PCNA needs multi-stage review (G0804). - Prerequisite. Check whether Classic Knowledge is already enabled. Turning on Lightning Knowledge can't be undone (G0801). - Content. Authoring, rewriting and the go-live article count are PCNA's (G0006, G0805). Content design is a discipline gap (G0806).

E09 — AI Grounding (Data Libraries / Data 360)

Business context: Casey and the CSR AI features need accurate, current answers from PCNA's own content.

Solution approach: two Agentforce Data Libraries, both built on Knowledge: - Casey's library: public, published articles only. - CSR library (Service Assistant and Service Replies): public and internal articles.

Supporting architecture: - What a library sets up. Building a library creates the Data 360 data streams, search index and retriever automatically. Data 360 has to be set up in the org, with a sandbox for testing (G0904). - Language. English only, which matches Service Assistant's indexing limit (G0907). - Acceptance. Answer quality is accepted against a PCNA-approved question set and pass rate (G0906). - Citations. Casey's answers can't cite source articles until PCNA has a public help-center URL (G0905).

E10 — CSR Productivity AI

Business context: CSRs handle cases, calls and, soon, chats without AI help.

Solution approach: at go-live, CSRs get: - Service Assistant in the case panel. - Work Summary and Conversation Catch-up. - Service Replies. - Article suggestions. - AI-drafted Knowledge articles. - A starter set of Next Best Action recommendations.

Reply Recommendations is offered as a later option. It needs about 1,000 closed CSR chats, and web chat is new (G1002). Whether it goes in a later phase is PCNA's decision.

Supporting architecture: - Service Assistant: - Grounded on the CSR library. - Instructions and subagents follow PCNA's procedures by case type, which PCNA supplies. - Reuses Casey's order-lookup actions (G1003, G1005). - Service Replies: grounded with the Agentforce Data Library rather than Service AI Grounding. Once a library is assigned, you can't switch back (G1008). - Article suggestions: keyword-based Suggested Articles at go-live, because AI article recommendations have no case history yet (G1007). AI-drafted articles go into the E08 approval process (G1009). - Next Best Action: a small starter set of recommendations through Flow-based strategies, whose use cases PCNA confirms (G1001). - Voice depends on Vonage. - Needs the live transcript: Service Assistant, catch-up and summaries during calls only work if Vonage writes the transcript into Salesforce during the call. - Works without it: chat and case-level features don't need the live transcript. - until Vonage confirms (G1004, G0205).

E11 — Outlook Integration

Business context: CSRs email PCNA staff and vendors about escalations from Outlook, outside the Email-to-Case queue. Those emails never reach the case.

Solution approach: the Outlook integration with Einstein Activity Capture, with "Sync Email as Salesforce Activity" turned on so emails are stored in core Salesforce, where CSRs and the AI can use them.

Supporting architecture: - Where emails are stored. Activity Capture stores email outside core Salesforce unless sync is on. - Linking to the case. No source confirms Activity Capture can link an email to a Case automatically. The fallback is reps relating emails to cases from the Outlook side panel. (G1101, G1102). - Excluded domains. Capture must not exclude PCNA's own domains, or it would drop the internal escalation emails (G1103). - Connected users. Only primary Microsoft 365 mailboxes in UPN format can connect, so shared or alias mailboxes won't work (G1107). - PCNA's part. PCNA gives Microsoft 365 tenant admin consent (G1109).

E02 — Vonage Partner Telephony and Consoles (Salesforce side)

Business context: the Five9 connector is being replaced. CSRs and supervisors need Vonage inside the Salesforce console.

Solution approach: - Connection. Enable partner telephony, repoint the contact-center connection to Vonage, and mirror its queues and users. - CSR console. Update the console layouts for CSRs. - Supervisor console. Embed Vonage's supervisor console in Omni-Channel Command Center. - Call data. Show the transcripts, recordings and sentiment that Vonage writes to Salesforce.

Supporting architecture: - Queue sync. Queues and users sync through Salesforce's standard queue-sync interfaces (QueueManager, QueueSetup), with Vonage as the master. Whether Vonage's connector supports this is (G0201). - Users and permissions. CSR and supervisor users are matched between Vonage and Salesforce, with permission sets for partner telephony (G0207). - Command Center embed. Embedding the Vonage supervisor console is an assumption that Vonage provides an embeddable component. (G0204). - Call data on records. Which records Vonage writes transcripts, recordings and sentiment to is confirmed with Vonage (G0205). - Cutover. The switch from Five9 to Vonage on the Salesforce side is planned with E12 (G0206, G1205).

E01 — Phase 0: Mobilization and Integration Design

Business context: the order systems, the Vonage behavior and the POC state are all unknown today.

Solution approach: - Order systems. Design the FDM4 and other order-system interfaces. - Vonage. Run the joint design for the SIP trunk, routing, availability and queue sync. - Casey. Confirm the proof-of-concept handover date and what it includes, check the POC can be promoted, and lock the order subtopic's design. - Knowledge. Lock the content scope. - Org. Inventory the current org.

Exit criteria and PCNA sign-off owners are defined at kickoff (G0101). The biggest output of Phase 0 is resizing E03, E04, E06 and E10 against real answers.

E12 — Program Management, Knowledge Transfer and Hypercare

Business context: 100 CSRs get a new softphone, chat channel, pods and AI assistance at once, across three delivery parties: PCNA, Salesforce PS and Vonage.

Solution approach: - Program management. Governance across all parties. - Knowledge transfer. Admin documentation and handover to PCNA's Salesforce admins. - Cutover. Coordinate the Vonage cutover, with a phased-versus-big-bang decision and a rollback plan. - Baseline. Capture baseline KPIs before Five9 is switched off. - Hypercare. Two weeks of post-go-live support.

PCNA owns change management and CSR classroom training (G0019). The fit with the Vonage cutover sets the critical path (G0008).

Delivery plan

PCNA Service Cloud 2026: Delivery Plan

Date: 2026-10-14 · Status: Draft. Scope assumptions are open until PCNA answers the shared questions.

Total program duration: 34 weeks (per user commitment): 32 weeks to go-live plus 2 weeks of hypercare. There is one go-live, aligned to the Vonage cutover.

The plan runs in six phases. Phase 0 settles the order-system and Vonage design. Phase 1 builds the foundations Casey needs: telephony, order lookup and Knowledge. Phase 2 takes over Casey from the Salesforce team, adds the order subtopic, and builds the grounding. Phase 3 puts Casey on voice and web chat and turns on the CSR AI features. Phase 4 runs UAT and cutover. Phase 5 is two weeks of post-go-live hypercare. PCNA runs change management and trains its 100 CSRs; Salesforce PS provides knowledge transfer and admin documentation.

Critical path: E01 Phase 0 → E06 Order Lookup → E03 Casey handover and order subtopic → E04 Casey Voice → E12 Cutover. A slip in the order-system design, or a Casey proof-of-concept handover that isn't ready when PS starts, moves the go-live.

Casey assumption (G0021): the Salesforce team builds and tests Casey, the out-of-the-box Agentforce Service Agent (case actions, Knowledge answers, identity verification, transfer to a CSR), as a sandbox POC before PS starts. PS doesn't build that agent. PS takes over the handover, promotes Casey to production, adds the "Where is my order?" subtopic and runs regression testing.

Phases

Phase 0: Mobilization and Integration Design (3 weeks)

Epics: E01 - Goal: mobilize the three parties (PCNA, Vonage and Salesforce) and close the gaps that set scope. - Order systems: name all three, confirm API access, and define how Casey works out which system holds an order and what "Where is my order?" returns. - Vonage joint design: the SIP trunk, queue and user sync, the handback to CSRs, and the routing split. - Done when: - The order-system design is signed off. - Vonage has confirmed in writing that it is a supported SIP partner, and the cutover date is set. - PCNA's Knowledge content inventory and authors are named. - Why a Phase 0: 114 gaps are open, and the scoping workbook already places order-system design in Phase 0.

Phase 1: Foundations: Telephony, Orders and Knowledge (10 weeks)

Epics: E02 Vonage partner telephony and consoles · E06 Order Lookup · E07 Case Management · E08 Knowledge · depends on: E01 - Goal: - Connect Vonage through Salesforce Voice partner telephony. - Sync Vonage queues and users and update the CSR and supervisor consoles. - Build the read-only order-status action with one adapter per order system. It goes first because it carries the most uncertainty. - Set up Knowledge and start converting CSR policy files into articles. Content is PCNA's longest-running task. - Add parent/child cases and case teams. - Done when: - Vonage is connected in a sandbox, with queue and user sync working. - The order action returns live results from all three systems. - The first articles are published through the approval process. - Case teams are live in testing.

Phase 2: Casey Handover and AI Grounding (6 weeks)

Epics: E03 Casey handover · E09 AI Grounding · E11 Outlook · depends on: E06, E08, Casey proof-of-concept handover - Goal: - Accept the Casey proof-of-concept handover and promote it from the sandbox to production. Data libraries can't be deployed from a sandbox, so each environment gets its own. - Once the handover is done, add the "Where is my order?" subtopic on the order action from E06, and review guardrails. - Configure the two Knowledge-based Data Libraries. - Deliver the Outlook integration. - Done when: - Casey is live in production and passes regression testing on the handed-over actions and the new order subtopic. - Grounding acceptance tests pass on both libraries. - Escalation emails land on the Case.

Phase 3: Channels and CSR Productivity AI (9 weeks)

Epics: E04 Casey Voice · E05 Web Chat and Web-to-Case · E10 CSR Productivity AI · depends on: E02, E03, E09 - Goal: - Put Casey on voice, with calls from Vonage over SIP. - Put Casey on live web chat through Enhanced Chat and launch web-to-case. - Turn on the CSR features: Service Assistant, Service Replies, Work Summary, Conversation Catch-up, Knowledge drafting and recommendations, and Next Best Action. - Done when: - Calls through Vonage reach Casey and hand back to a CSR with context. - Chat escalates from Casey to a CSR. - Casey passes regression testing again on voice and chat. - Pilot CSRs accept the AI features.

Phase 4: Stabilization and Cutover (4 weeks)

Epics: E12 · depends on: E04, E05, E10 - Goal: - Run end-to-end UAT across all three parties. - Cut over on the Vonage go-live. - Hand over to PCNA admins: knowledge transfer and admin documentation. PCNA delivers CSR training and change management (G0019). - Done when: - UAT is signed off. - Go-live happens on the Vonage date. - Program management runs across every phase. It sits here only for scheduling.

Phase 5: Hypercare (2 weeks)

Epics: E12 · depends on: go-live - Goal: post-go-live support. Triage and fix production issues across Casey, voice, chat and the CSR AI features, and tune Casey on real conversations. - Done when: the hypercare exit criteria are met and support has passed to PCNA.

Standard Processes (all phases)

  • Testing: each phase has its own system testing. Agent work adds conversation test sets and grounding acceptance tests. End-to-end UAT, including Vonage, runs in Phase 4.

  • Task-level gains come from published 2022–2026 studies. A realization factor, chosen by project shape, converts them to project level. It accounts for Amdahl's law (only part of the effort speeds up), review overhead and work that AI doesn't move.
  • Honest range for coding: controlled trials range from −19% (METR 2025 [1], experienced open-source developers) to +21% (Paradis/Google 2024 [4], complex enterprise work) to +55% (Peng/GitHub 2022 [3], a small new-build lab task).   • AI model capability is advancing faster than real-world workflow gains (Stanford HAI 2026 [9]). That gap is why project-level bands stay in the ~10–25% range.
  • Three of the four readiness signals are unscored and held at the default.
  • Bands are qualitative and specific to this project. They imply no hours, FTE or cost.

0001 — Vonage replaces Five9 and connects through Salesforce partner telephony; Casey takes voice calls over SIP

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: accepted (ratified 2026-10-13) · Source: Salesforce-recommended

Context

PCNA is replacing Five9 with Vonage Contact Center as its telephony platform. Vonage owns IVR, call routing, recording, transcription, the outbound dialer and workforce management. Salesforce PS owns the Service Cloud side, including Casey, an Agentforce Service Agent that replaces PCNA's current "Clara" AI agent on voice and web chat. Open CTI is deprecated and retiring in February 2028, and it is unavailable for new Agentforce Service orgs. Agentforce Voice requires, at minimum, Salesforce Voice with Telephony Providers to be enabled.

Decision

  • The Vonage integration uses Salesforce Voice with Telephony Providers (partner telephony), never Open CTI.
  • Casey receives calls from Vonage over SIP. SIP passes full call context and needs no extra telecom licensing, but it is limited to vetted partners and requires a SIP-over-TLS trunk between Vonage's session border controller (SBC) and Salesforce's.
  • Vonage remains the call router ("one brain"). Salesforce mirrors Vonage queues and users and repoints the contact-center connection from Five9 to Vonage.

Consequences

  • Every voice and console epic assumes the partner-telephony model. Open CTI softphone work is out of scope.
  • Dependency: Vonage must be a supported Agentforce Voice SIP partner (open question G0002). This was assumed on 2026-10-13; Vonage still has to confirm it in writing. If it isn't, the fallback is Dynamic Routing, which works with any partner that can make an API call but doesn't support voice call recording. That fallback reopens this ADR.
  • Vonage owns the SIP trunk and its partner-side configuration. Salesforce PS owns the Salesforce-side channel, the Omni-Channel flows to Casey and the escalation path to a rep.

Grounds

  • Salesforce team, 2026-10-13 discovery interview: "We will use partner telephony b/c open CTI is discontinued"; "SIP telephony is assumed"; "customer is replacing Five 9s with Vonage."
  • Scoping workbook, Supervisor row: "Assuming 'Queue Mirror' + SIP Trunks for Agentforce Voice capabilities"; Case Routing row: "PCNA wants 1 brain and Vonage is proposing external routing feature."
  • Retirement date: the user gave Feb 2027 in conversation. The KB atom KA-5475 (official) says February 2028. This ADR uses the documented date. The decision is the same either way.
